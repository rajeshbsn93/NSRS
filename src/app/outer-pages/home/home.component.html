ABOUT NSRS

National Sports Repository System (NSRS) is a digital MIS, which envisages to provide a comprehensive digital solution for all the major stakeholders such as Athletes, Coaches, Sports Scientists, Sports Training Centers, Federations and Administrators. It is an open platform of sports eco-system where Athletes, Coaches and Academies can register themselves. On successful registration, an Unique ID is generated, which is used for accessing the NSRS.

NSRS ON MOBILE

The NSRS mobile application is currently in active development phase, and can be downloaded from Google Play Store.
